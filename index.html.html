Dhaka Dine & Dash

Keyboard: arrow keys or A/D to move, Up/W/Space to jump, J or Z to punch, K or C for the special move (it fills as you land hits), U or V to kick, S or Down to block, L or Shift to dodge-roll. Hit 3 in a row for a combo. You have 3 lives, and a red can gives a speed boost. On a phone, use the buttons. Tap the game screen to start.
